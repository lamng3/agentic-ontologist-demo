What runs where

Everything runs in your browser, on the two real ontologies of the Mouse–Human track. The Agentic Ontologist itself is Python and is not run here: this page recomputes the evidence tier with a port of its name index (TF-IDF over character n-grams, as scikit-learn computes it) and of the matching agent's retrieval and the controller's rule, and checks the result against the Python. The local and API models are not hosted: Ask my model uses a model you run: Ollama on this computer is detected automatically, or you can load a tiny model that runs in your browser on WebGPU (a few hundred megabytes, kept by your browser), or point it at another endpoint.
